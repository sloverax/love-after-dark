# Love After Dark — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Love After Dark Discord application ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the App.

By using Love After Dark, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Love After Dark may collect limited information provided through Discord that is necessary for the App to function.

This may include:

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs
- Discord role information
- Relationship status selections made through the App
- App configuration and preferences
- Command and interaction information necessary to process App features
- Basic diagnostic or error information

The exact information collected may depend on the features you use.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Love After Dark does not intentionally collect:

- Discord passwords
- Payment or banking information
- Private messages unrelated to interactions with the App
- Sensitive personal information unless explicitly required for a feature and disclosed to the user

Never provide your Discord password or other sensitive account credentials to Love After Dark.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information collected by Love After Dark may be used to:

- Provide the App's features
- Manage relationship statuses and related functionality
- Process Discord commands and interactions
- Maintain server-specific configurations
- Diagnose technical problems
- Prevent abuse or misuse
- Improve the App

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users, prevent abuse, or protect the security and operation of Love After Dark.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information maintained by Love After Dark from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No electronic system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Love After Dark, provide requested features, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data that is no longer necessary may be deleted.

## 7. Data Deletion and User Requests

Users may request deletion or correction of information associated with them that is stored by Love After Dark.

Requests may be submitted through the official Love After Dark support channels.

We may request your Discord User ID or other information necessary to locate the applicable App data.

Deleting certain information may prevent some Love After Dark features from functioning correctly.

## 8. Discord

Love After Dark operates using Discord's platform and services.

Discord may separately collect and process information according to its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Love After Dark does not control Discord's collection or use of information.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Love After Dark is intended only for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements.

We do not knowingly collect information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Love After Dark changes.

When material changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this policy will be updated.

Continued use of the App after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy.

## 11. Contact

Questions, privacy concerns, or data requests may be submitted through the official Love After Dark support channels.
